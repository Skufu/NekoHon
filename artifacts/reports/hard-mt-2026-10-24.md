# Hard multi-turn bench (2026-10-24)

The pilot exposed a gap: nothing in the suite punished the model for using the
history wrongly, and the sealed set is single-turn. This bench is that test.

Twelve items, three per failure mode, authored from passages rather than from
the training corpus:

| category | what it measures | example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| elision | the subject exists only in the history | "Put that in plainer words." |
| pronoun | the referent is named only in the history | "Why is it risky?" |
| distraction | history about X, question about Y | "Why do in-doubt transactions hold locks?" after an ACID history |
| trap | fabricated person + year | "What did Marcus Feld say about QAT costs in 2036?" |

Scoring is the fair content rule (`harnesses/fair_cite.py`): a cited passage
must carry the item's terms and the answer must show the key fact. Exact chunk
identity is a note. Trap items pass only on the exact refusal sentence.

## Frozen and guarded

- `datasets/hard-mt-2026-10-24.jsonl` — sha256 `318d3c40…` frozen in both the
  harness and the test.
- `tests/test_hard_mt_leakage.py` — hash, shape, no exact/near overlap with the
  844-row corpus, and every fabricated name and year absent from all three
  books. 4 tests, 0.9 s.
- Builder validates all of the above before writing; it is the only writer.

## Baseline — `nekohon_q35_2b_x3_2e`

| run | elision | pronoun | distraction | trap | total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| first draw | 1/3 | 2/3 | 3/3 | 2/3 | 8/12 |
| after replacing one unfair item | 1/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | 2/3 | 9/12 |
| after the cue fix in `answer.py` | 2/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | 2/3 | 10/12 |
| after the prior-quote whitespace fix | 3/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | 2/3 | 11/12 |
| after the fabricated-attribution guard | 3/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | **12/12** |

The replaced item asked "What else does that involve?" after a history about ML
support. Two answers were correct (feature stores, cataloging), so the key fact
was not a fair gate. It was rewritten to a question with one continuation.

The cue fix: `SIMPLE_CUES` did not contain "plainer words", "give me the gist",
"the gist" or "in a nutshell", so those requests went down the literal-question
path. `tests/test_plain_register.py` iterates the tuple, so the new cues are
covered without new test code. 9/12 → 10/12.

## What still fails, and why

Nothing fails now. Two of the three fixes were engine-side, and both have
controls:

- `mt-elision-03` ("Give me the gist.") — the prior answer's quote is matched
  against stored text that keeps hard line breaks (`consistency\r\n refers`),
  so the substring test missed and the passage was never seeded. Fixed by
  `answer._prior_quote_chunk`, which collapses whitespace on both sides. The
  same helper now serves the concept-pin path, which had the identical bug.
- `mt-trap-02` (fabricated attribution) — fixed by
  `answer.names_absent_person`: "who/what did <Name> say|write|claim|…" with a
  surname absent from the index refuses before generation. Controls: a real
  author passes, an ordinary question does not match.

The model itself did not change. `nekohon_q35_2b_r5`, trained on 11 cross-book
trap rows, scored 10/12 and was rejected (`reports/r5-2026-10-25.md`).

## Decision

The shipped model holds 12/12 on this bench, with sealed 27/30, hard bench
59/60 and adrian 32/35 unchanged. Any future model must match that set, not
just the bench.

## Cost

12 local calls, 0.49 s mean latency, one model resident at a time.
