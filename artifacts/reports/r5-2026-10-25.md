# Round 5 — cross-book trap rows, and the rejection (2026-10-25)

Hypothesis: the one hard-mt failure (`mt-trap-02`, a fabricated attribution on a
quantization passage) is a coverage gap — the 844-row corpus is fde-only. Add
traps from the other two books and retrain.

Result: **rejected.** The new corpus ties on sealed and loses a bench item. The
trap stayed unlearned. The fix that worked was a rule, not a row.

## Corpus

| source | rows | note |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `quote-refuse-2026-10-17.train-x3.jsonl` | 844 | shipped corpus |
| `pilot-rows-2026-10-24.jsonl` (premise_trap + simplify only) | 15 | audited pilot rows; entity-relation excluded (11/18 and 9/18 rejected) |
| `trap-crossbook-rows-2026-10-24.jsonl` | 11 | 6 quantization + 5 ddia; one answered row dropped by the assembler |
| **total after dedupe** | **870** | 158 refusals, 18.2%, sha256 `584b49e1…` |

The 12 cross-book questions were generated by Qwen3.8-27B on one A100 session.
11/12 came back as the exact refusal; `trap-xb-05` answered and was dropped.
No model audit was run for these: the builder proves each name and year absent
from all three books, and the target is the exact refusal, so there is nothing
semantic left to judge.

## Training

Same recipe as x3_2e: r16/alpha32, lr 2e-4, responses-only, seed 3407,
validation on the frozen 76-question list, 2 epochs (the 3-epoch point was
already shown to overfit on this corpus size).

| epoch | train loss | eval loss |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.5231 | 0.4726 |
| 2 | 0.2186 | 0.4777 |

Eval loss rises 0.0051 on the second epoch — same shape as x3_2e (0.4785 →
0.4830), so the 2-epoch stop point is still right.

## Gates

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| hard-mt (12) | 11/12 (12/12 after the engine fix) | **10/12** | worse |
| sealed (30) | 27/30 | **27/30** | tie |
| hard bench (60) | 59/60 | not run | — |
| adrian (35) | 32/35 | not run | — |

`r5` failed `mt-distract-03`: it quoted a BART railway-gauge sentence in a
chapter-12 passage when asked what reprocessing does. `mt-trap-02` still
answered the fabricated attribution.

Stopped at the bench gate: the pre-registered rule was "10/12 or better and no
trade elsewhere", and a tie on sealed does not buy back a bench loss.

## What the failure says

Three rounds now (r3, r4, r5) have added rows and moved failures around instead
of removing them. This is the fourth time a longer corpus costs an unrelated
item. More rows are not the lever for this behaviour.

## What actually fixed the trap

`answer.names_absent_person`: a question that attributes a claim to a person
whose surname appears nowhere in the index is refused before generation. It is
narrow — it needs "who/what did <Name> say|write|claim|…" and an absent surname —
and it has its own controls: a real author must not be refused, an ordinary
question must not match.

Shipped model after the guard: **12/12** on hard-mt, with sealed 27/30, hard
bench 59/60 and adrian 32/35 unchanged.

## Decision

`nekohon_q35_2b_r5` stays on disk as a record and is **not** the default. The
shipped answerer remains `nekohon_q35_2b_x3_2e`; the improvement this round is
engine-side.

## Cost

One A100 training session (~35 min including GGUF export), one A100 generation
session (12 prompts), local gates. No default changed.
