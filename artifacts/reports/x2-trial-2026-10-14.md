# smallbook_x2 live trial — fragment-pattern observations (2026-10-14)

The user tests with real-world sloppiness: fragments ("what is", "Location"),
greetings ("hey", "warrup"), bare topics ("DE Lifecycle"), comparisons with
the order flipped ("DDR5 vs ddr4" / "ddr4 vs ddr5"), trailing letters
("speed of ddr5s"). That surface is where the finetune is weakest. Raw
repro of all ten queries on both models through the app seam (answer mode):
`probe-trial-queries-2026-10-14.{json,log}`.

## Classification

- **Model warts** (x2 false-refuses where the 7B answers, all in-book):
  Location, DE Lifecycle, ddr4-vs-ddr5 — same disease as CRU/Codd: the
  decision margin sits a hair from refusal on any form not memorized
  exactly.
- **Engine items** (model-independent, backlog — fix after the session so
  trial attribution stays clean): "hey" -> `Read as: "they"` over-repair on
  greetings; "ddr5s" plural not repaired by either model; "what is"
  degenerate input answered with a passage.
- **Both-models-bad**: "what is", "speed of ddr5s". The 7B is not clean on
  this surface either (stray `Not in this book.` inside an answer).

## Effect on the next session design (proposed)

Slot A (x2c) widens to this pattern: ~30 of the 167 answer rows swapped in
place (counts and ratio held) to perturbed forms — lowercase fragments,
order flips, abbreviations/plurals. Slot B stays the seed control.
Acceptance probe for x2c = this probe's ten queries plus the batteries;
target: match the 7B on the three model warts, keep refusals 2/2 and loops
at zero.

## Pre-registered swap gate (written 2026-10-14, BEFORE x2c trains)

Committed before seeing any x2c / seed-control numbers so the pass/fail
rule cannot be negotiated after the fact. Temperature 0 throughout;
fragment/loop checks count draws (>= 10), not single runs; recordings
land in dated probe logs.

For a candidate to replace the trial default:

1. Refusals: France + iops exact (2/2); no in-book false refusal in the
   sealed fragment suite beyond the 7B's own misses on the same suite.
2. Fragment suite (new file, sealed, never trained): per-class pass rate
   >= the 7B's (classes: fragment, greeting, bare topic, order flip,
   trailing letter). The ten queries above are NOT the suite — they are
   visible targets.
3. No loops: 0 length-stops in the capped sweep (all 21 battery questions
   x 5 draws) and in the fragment suite (x 10 draws).
4. Battery: pin-pass rate not worse than x2's recorded 18-19/21, compared
   at equal draw counts, not single-run counts.
5. Quant check: the candidate's warts persist at high precision (q8_0
   export of the same run) — a wart that vanishes at q8_0 is a
   quantization verdict, not a training verdict.
6. Revert rehearsal: `ANSWER_MODEL=qwen2.5:7b` starts and passes the
   battery once during the trial.
