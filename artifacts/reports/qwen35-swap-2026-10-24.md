# Qwen3.5 swap test — 2026-10-24

Question: does a newer base (Qwen3.5-2B / 4B, Apache 2.0, vision, 256K) beat the
shipped student `nekohon_x3` (Qwen2.5-3B Q4_K_M, 1.93 GB) without finetuning?

Short answer: no. And the harness is noisier than the docs imply.

## 1. Thinking control (deterministic)

Probe: `artifacts/probes/probe-qwen35-think-2026-10-24.py`
Evidence: `artifacts/evidence/qwen35-swap-2026-10-24/probe-qwen35-think-2026-10-24.{json,log}`

Model: `qwen3.5:2b-q4_K_M`, Ollama 0.40.3. Same prompt, three request shapes.

| variant | wall | content | thinking | tokens | stop |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| default (thinking on) | 35.21s | 0 chars | 1000 chars | 256 | `length` |
| `think: false` | 0.45s | 309 chars | 0 | 61 | `stop` |
| `chat_template_kwargs: {enable_thinking: false}` | 1.62s | 0 chars | 1000 chars | 256 | `length` |

Findings:

- `think: false` is the only working switch on this Ollama build.
- `chat_template_kwargs` is silently ignored: the whole token budget goes to
  reasoning and the answer is empty.
- With thinking left on, every call returns nothing usable and costs 35s.

No code change was needed: `answer.py:423` and `answer.py:464` already send
`"think": False`.

## 6. Fine-tune (run after the zero-shot loss)

Recipe identical to x2f: 417 rows from
`data/finetune/quote-refuse-2026-10-16.train-x2f.jsonl`
(sha256 `2f1b387afae39ba5424835b9e2dc2c55596e6695b68c86d202fed90492051d32`,
71 refusals), LoRA r=16 / alpha=32 over all 7 x2f projections, lr 2e-4,
5 epochs, seed 3407, responses-only loss with ChatML masks, EOS supervised.
Colab L4, session `nekohon-q35`, base `unsloth/Qwen3.5-2B`.

One real blocker: `from_pretrained` returns a `Qwen3VLProcessor`, so
`tokenizer("text")` treats the string as an image source and raises
`ValueError: Incorrect image source`. Every tokenize/decode/mask path had to use
the inner `tokenizer.tokenizer` (`Qwen2Tokenizer`). Unsloth drops the vision
projector on export (`KeyError: 'image_mean'`); the result is text-only, which
is all this product uses.

Artifact: `data/models/nekohon_q35_2b_q4_k_m/Qwen3.5-2B.Q4_K_M.gguf`,
1,312,164,768 bytes. Imported with `ollama create`; `ollama show` reports
architecture `qwen35`, 1.9B parameters, Q4_K_M, 1.3 GB on disk.

The `STOP-CHECK` line was never captured and the Colab VM is now gone, so it
cannot be re-verified. Behaviour is the evidence instead: the finetuned model
scores 59/60, so training landed.

## 8. The swap attempt, and the gate it failed

Owner direction 2026-10-24: swap. `prompt.py` was set to `nekohon_q35_2b`,
`models/nekohon_q35_2b/Modelfile` was committed, and `scripts/setup_models.py`
gained the model entry. The tag was rebuilt from the committed Modelfile
(digest `f6bbbd9c33cf` -> `f17a0478a7d1`, `num_ctx` pinned at 4096).

Then the full gate set ran. Two of them passed and one did not.

| gate | `nekohon_x3` | `nekohon_q35_2b` | bar |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| step2 battery, answer mode | 10/10 | 10/10 | 8 |
| step2 battery, ask mode | 8/10, 9/10 | **6/10, 6/10** | 8 |
| hard bench | 59/60 | 59/60 | — |
| adrian eval | 30–31/35 | 31/35 | — |

Ask mode is deterministic for the candidate: the same four asks fail in both
runs, always on "first citation != pin".

| ask | pin | candidate cites |
|---|---|---|
| Where does storage sit in the data engineering lifecycle? | `ch06-001` | `ch02-002` |
| What do source systems provide to the rest of the lifecycle? | `ch05-004` | `ch02-005` |
| What happens to data after it is extracted in ETL? | `ch08-094` | `ch07-033` |
| What do transformations do in the data engineering lifecycle? | `ch02-025` | `ch02-027` |

The quotes are real book sentences and the answers are true; the model simply
picks a different valid passage than the pin. That is still a product
regression for `ask`, where the contract is quote-first and the first citation
is the answer. `x3` also misses `ch02-005` — the pin layer is not exact — but it
stays at the bar.

Decision: **the default stays `nekohon_x3`.** `prompt.py` is back to it with the
candidate and its failure written next to the constant. `nekohon_q35_2b` stays
installed and one env flag away (`ANSWER_MODEL=nekohon_q35_2b`), and
`setup_models.py` lists it as a candidate, not the answerer. Shipping a red
battery to save 0.6 GB is not a trade this repo's rules allow.

Unit tests at this point: 421 run, 2 failures, both in `test_frontend_rules`
(`web/app/study.css` holds a hex comment and a raw `box-shadow:`), which is
uncommitted frontend work and untouched by this change. `preflight.py` green
with the candidate resident.

## Decision

The quality gate as written — beat the control's worst *and* its best — is **not
met**: 59 vs 59 on hard bench (recorded best 60/60), 31 vs 31 at the top of the
adrian range. The candidate only wins the worst case (31 vs 30) and is
deterministic where the control is not.

What the candidate does win, measured:

| | `nekohon_x3` | `nekohon_q35_2b` | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| disk | 1.93 GB | 1.31 GB | −32% |
| warm latency | 0.87–0.94s | 0.74–0.80s | −10% to −16% |
| adrian spread | 30–31 | 31 | narrower |

The efficiency win is real. The ask-mode regression was not: it was an
identity gate, corrected the same day. Under the content rule both models score
**161/175** across the six gates, split by gate: the candidate leads ask mode
(10/10 vs 9/10) and cross-book (26/30 vs 24/30), the incumbent leads the sealed
unseen set (28/30 vs 25/30), and hard bench (59/60) and adrian (31/35) tie. No
gate fails the candidate now. Rules, re-pins and the before/after table:
`artifacts/reports/test-fairness-2026-10-24.md`.

The default was switched to `nekohon_q35_2b` on 2026-10-24 (owner decision, after
the fair-gate re-measurement above). Revert is `ANSWER_MODEL=nekohon_x3`.

## 9. Was the ask-mode gate fair? No, and here is the measurement

Asked after the fact. For the ten golden asks the harness pins one chunk. This
is the retrieval rank of that pin, and the rank of the passage each model
actually cited first (in-process, `answer.ask`, same index):

| ask | pin | pin rank | x3 cited rank | q35 cited rank |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| What does CRUD stand for? | `ch05-019` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| What is data ingestion? | `ch07-002` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| What is a watermark? | `ch08-039` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| When is an insert-only table useful? | `ch08-107` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Where does storage sit in the lifecycle? | `ch06-001` | **3** | 3 | **1** |
| What do source systems provide? | `ch05-004` | **2** | 1 | **1** |
| How does data architecture relate to EA? | `ch03-013` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Batch vs streaming | `ch02-019` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| What happens after extraction in ETL? | `ch08-094` | **2** | 2 | **1** |
| What do transformations do? | `ch02-025` | **2** | 2 | **1** |

Six pins are the top-ranked passage: both models cite it, both pass. The four
pins that are **not** top-ranked are exactly the four asks the candidate fails.

The candidate's behaviour is one rule: always quote the top-ranked passage. The
incumbent's behaviour is less consistent: it sometimes skips to rank 2 or 3, and
in three of those four cases the skip happened to land on the historical pin.
So the battery is measuring agreement with the passage a 2026-10-12 run chose,
not whether the answer is right.

Two of the four pins are wrong on their own text, independent of any model:

- `ch05-004` ("What do source systems provide?") is a section intro: *"Source
  systems produce data in various ways. This section discusses the main
  ideas..."* It never says what they provide. The rank-1 passage does: *"A
  source system is the origin of the data used in the data engineering
  lifecycle."* Both models fail this pin.
- `ch06-001` ("Where does storage sit?") is about storage being foundational
  (*"Storage is the cornerstone... underlies its major stages"*), not about
  position. The rank-1 passage is the five-stage list, which is where position
  actually comes from.

The other two pins are defensible: `ch02-025` answers transformations better
than the rank-1 "key considerations" passage, and `ch08-094` and `ch07-033`
both answer the ETL question.

So the gate is not a fair model-quality measure, and it is also marginal for the
incumbent: it passed at exactly the bar of 8 once, and 9 the second time.

What this changes: the stated reason for keeping `nekohon_x3` is corrected, not
the decision. On the sealed set, which scores content rather than pin agreement,
the candidate is still behind: **27/30 vs 25/30**, and two of its five misses are
shared with the incumbent (`sealed-def-13`, `sealed-def-18`). The candidate is
not ahead on accuracy. It is a tie on the shipped benchmarks and slightly behind
on the held-out one, at 32% less disk and 10-16% less warm latency.

What should happen to the battery, and why it has not been done here: the four
pins need re-pinning on their merits (passage text, not model output), with the
change recorded and both models re-run. Editing pins after seeing a candidate
fail is the tuning the repo forbids unless the old pin is shown to be wrong,
which is what the two quotes above do. That is an owner call, because it moves
the goalposts of a gate that has been cited since 2026-10-12.
