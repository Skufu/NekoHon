# x2e verdict — the 27B student, the engine alignment, what was adopted (2026-10-15)

## Headline

- **An engine bug was found and fixed.** Answer mode served a prompt the
  student was never trained on. Serving the trained (ask-shaped) prompt in
  answer mode lifted the shipped x2d from 111 to **160/160** on the sealed
  suite; x2e went 40 → 150; the 7B went 140 → 160. Zero loops everywhere.
  Adopted — a tactic change, with every gate re-run.
- **x2e is not adopted.** It fails the pre-registered battery item (ask-mode
  golden 7/10 < 8) and false-refuses two of the user's visible-ten queries.
  x2d stays the default.

## The bug, precisely

- Every training row (x2d's and x2e's) carries the **ask seam**:
  `SYSTEM` ("Quote first. Start the quote with [Ch. N]...") + the "Copy one
  sentence from a passage" reminder.
- The engine's answer mode served `SYSTEM_ANSWER` ("a few sentences... in
  your own words") — the opposite instruction.
- x2d survived it by over-memorizing (train loss 0.006: it recited passage
  text regardless of the prompt). x2e (loss 0.035) followed the served
  prompt, paraphrased, and failed the verbatim check — its retry
  continuation `[Ch. 7] To understand how data is created...` contains no
  quotable sentence, so the retry could not validate.
- Fix: `answer.respond` and `_retry_uncited` send `SYSTEM` + `user_message`
  (byte-identical to the training prompts). `SYSTEM_ANSWER` +
  `answer_user_message` remain for the plain-register draft path. Three
  tests updated (`test_free_answer`, `test_canonical`, `test_plain_register`).

- x2e's single suite miss: `benefits of data meshs` false-refuses 10/10.
- x2e's three ask-mode golden misses are **pin mismatches with valid
  quotes** (`ch05-083` vs pin `ch02-005`; `ch02-018` vs `ch02-019`;
  `ch02-025` vs `ch02-027`) — a curation question, but the pre-registered
  rule is the rule.

## Why x2e is not adopted

1. Pre-registered item 1 fails (ask golden 7/10, bar 8).
2. False refusals on two visible-ten items — the reader-first rule counts a
   false refusal as broken output.

## What the 27B data did buy (kept for the next iteration)

- Better novel-form behavior: owner probe 17 vs 13 PASS; the answer-mode
  coherence item x2d misses (`endpoint in streaming ingestion`) is answered
  correctly by x2e, from the right passage (`ch02-024`).
- `smallbook_x2e` (digest `b97723309c21`) + LoRA zip + run log stay on disk
  and in `evidence/x2e-session-2026-10-15/`.

## Recorded costs and backlog

- x2d answer-mode coherence is 21/22: the endpoint item quotes a less apt
  sentence. Backlog: prefer quotes carrying the question's anchor words when
  several passages qualify (the ask mode's `anchor_words` machinery).
- Next iteration candidates: a pin-curation audit (are the 27B's chosen
  chunks better than the old pins?); the false refusals (typo class,
  `DE Lifecycle`, `ddr4 vs ddr5`) that persist despite training rows;
  possibly more epochs on the 27B data.

## Revert

`ANSWER_MODEL=smallbook_x2d` (default); `=qwen2.5:7b` or `=smallbook_x2`
revert the model. The alignment is two prompt call sites in `answer.py`;
reverting it would restore the 111/160 behavior — not recommended.
